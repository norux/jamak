Download only the selected, pinned PP-OCRv5 or PP-OCRv6 detection and recognition model archives from PaddlePaddle's official paddle-model-ecology.bj.bcebos.com host, whose archive responses lack CORS headers. Every archive is checked for its exact size and SHA-256 and cached locally. This permission does not grant access to ordinary websites. Image pixels, screenshots, OCR text, audio and translations are never sent to this host; OCR and translation inference run on the user's device.
